# Desktop assets and attribution

`assets/themes/Catppuccin-Mauve-Dark` is the GTK theme used on the source machine.
`assets/icons/Papirus` and `Papirus-Dark` are stored together because the dark
variant links into the base tree. Internal relative links are preserved.

Papirus upstream: https://github.com/PapirusDevelopmentTeam/papirus-icon-theme
Catppuccin project: https://github.com/catppuccin
The installed GTK theme snapshot did not include a standalone license file;
no new license or authorship claim is applied to third-party assets here.
Original copyright and licenses continue to apply.

The thirteen wallpaper filenames retain their Wallhaven identifiers. Their
source pages can be found at `https://wallhaven.cc/w/<identifier>`. Copyright
belongs to the respective artists; no blanket redistribution license is
asserted by this dotfiles repository. Review the original asset terms before
republishing them elsewhere.

Breeze cursor and fallback icons, fonts, and program-provided application icons
remain package dependencies. Unused Ant/Colloid/Papirus-Light themes, unrelated
game launchers/icons, and generated icon caches are not needed by this desktop.
Custom Wlogout icons and the green SVG versions are under `wlogout/icons`.
Wi-Fi/audio microphone icons and HyprCapture UI icons are inside their source
snapshots, alongside their upstream licensing material.
